# TEROS 11 retest on Jolteon

On November 16, 2026, Jolteon on COM24 ran the November 13 11:37:05 SDI-12 firmware with the TEROS 11 rewired. Discovery, acknowledgement, identification, and measurement were tried before and after a console-controlled sensor power cycle. The console enabled the 12 V boost and reported A3 high. Sensor voltage under load was not measured.

```text
SDI ?! attempts=9 | No response start bit.
SDI 0! attempts=9 | No response start bit.
SDI 0I! attempts=9 | No response start bit.
SDI 0M! attempts=3 | Invalid start bit.
```

The short pulse occurred once. After the power cycle and during three further measurement attempts, `0M!` timed out after nine sends. Model-selected `sdi read` also timed out at identification. The test yielded no valid reply.

For the next test, the `sdi trace` candidate sends one command per invocation and reports the first HIGH seen by the Feather and the number of rejected short pulses. The candidate was compiled and tested with simulated input. Hardware measurements at J_SDI12 DATA and U7 A/B are needed to locate the failure.

## Trace firmware retest

The November 16 19:58:17 candidate was flashed to Jolteon and the uploader
verified 225,388 bytes. After a sensor power off/on cycle, five single-shot
discovery commands, retrying discovery, and both single-shot and retrying
identification saw no start bit. Two of three single-shot measurement commands
also saw no start bit. One recorded:

```text
SDI 0M! attempts=1 | Unexpected reply character. | first RX HIGH 8 us after RX start
```

That pulse was present much earlier than the SDI-12 response window. No valid
frame was decoded. The sensor supply and digital bus voltages were not measured.
The next hardware check is a passive startup capture at J_SDI12 pin 2, triggered
by A3 asserting during `sdi power on`, with the TEROS 11 wired. METER documents a
DDI startup burst at the factory address 0 when DDI output is enabled. A burst
at U7 B that is missing at U7 A isolates the receive path.

## Passive startup and local data-path diagnostics

The final `sdi-path` firmware (November 16 20:09:20) was flashed and verified
on Jolteon COM24. It retains `sdi startup` and `sdi trace` from the earlier
diagnostic build and adds `sdi path`.

- The A3 boost enable read back low and high as commanded. D10 read back high
  on transmit and low on receive. These are MCU pad checks. Supply voltages and
  the levels at the components were not measured.
- Three passive power-up captures on the `sdi-startup` build sampled D9 for
  650 ms each, with no transmitted command. All saw zero transitions and a
  maximum sample gap of 8 us. A DDI burst is conditional on sensor
  configuration and actual power-up.
- Four local pulse tests on the final build saw D9 high in transmit and
  immediately after changing direction. The first low sample was about
  771 to 783 us after release. Three tests still read low at the following
  sample. The fourth read high at that sample, consistent with a transition
  near the switching threshold. The repeated high after turnaround shows a
  local pulse can travel through U7 and return, at the board's own transmit
  voltage. The test does not measure the sensor-side voltage or prove that
  U7 will recognize the lower TEROS output.
- Discovery, identification and measurement produced no valid frames. The
  remaining checks are TEROS supply voltage under load and a field-side
  waveform at J_SDI12 pin 2 or U7 B when the sensor should respond.
