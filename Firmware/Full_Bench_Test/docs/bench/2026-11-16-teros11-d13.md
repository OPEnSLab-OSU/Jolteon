# Rewire v9 D13 and TEROS 11 validation

On November 16, 2026, Jolteon on COM24 was flashed with the D13 bench build.
The uploader verified 226,924 bytes. The application reported compile time
`Nov 16 2026 21:00:37`.

The Rewire v9 schematic routes `12V_EN` to Feather D13 (header position 25),
`SDI_EN` to D10 and `LOW_SDI12` to D9. The sketch disables Hypnos status LED
control when the boost uses D13. D13 read low at startup and after
`rails on` initialized Hypnos. It tracked `rails 12 on/off` and
`sdi power on`. The rail GPIO was left high after testing.

Initial discovery and address 0 commands timed out. After the operator
asked for a fresh attempt, discovery returned address 1 and two complete
measurement cycles passed. The cause of the change between attempts was
not observed.

```text
12V_EN (D13)=1
sdi ?!    -> 1
sdi 1I!   -> 113METER   TER11 108T11-00000112
sdi set teros11 1
sdi read -> M: 10012, D: 1+1845.85+22.1
sdi read -> M: 10012, D: 1+1849.91+22.1
```

This verifies end-to-end SDI-12 communication with this sensor. The boost
output voltage and signal margins were not measured. During the initial
power-control test, Hypnos reported the RTC had lost power and seeded it
from the sketch compile time.
